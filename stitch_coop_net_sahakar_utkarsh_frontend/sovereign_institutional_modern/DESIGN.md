## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the poise, accountability, and clarity required of institutional digital public infrastructure and cooperative federation networks. The visual language blends authoritative governance with civic warmth—projecting operational solidity, legal certainty, and forward-looking momentum.

The design movement is **Corporate / Modern** elevated by precise GovTech ergonomics:
- **Authority without Coldness:** Deep, stabilizing indigos establish structural gravity, while purposeful ochre accents punctuate civic agency, achievements, and milestones.
- **Architectural Legibility:** Dense data sets, audit trails, and multi-tier workflows are organized through scannable visual anchors, crisp micro-borders, and high-contrast typographic hierarchy.
- **Target Audience:** Cooperative board members, federation administrators, regulatory auditors, and ground-level operators who demand zero ambiguity, rapid transaction comprehension, and rock-solid reliability across desktop consoles and field devices.

## Colors

The palette leverages a calibrated sovereign triad alongside deep-ink typography and layered slate structural tones:

- **Primary (`#1E3A8A` - Regal Indigo):** Represents the executive backbone. Applied to persistent navigation frames, top-level brand zones, structural action hubs, and verified status containers.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Warm Ochre / Marigold):** The energetic civic marker. Reserved for active tab underlines, milestone callouts, high-priority notifications, progress rings, and audit flag highlights. Use sparingly to maintain urgency and focus.
- **Tertiary (`#3B82F6` - Steel Blue):** The functional utility driver. Directs eye tracking toward interactive links, focus indicator rings, secondary actions, informational badges, and digital verification seals.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0F172A` - Deep Ink):** Delivers superior contrast (minimum 15:1 against surfaces) across all headlines, body copy, and high-density financial tables.
- **Neutral Light (`#F1F5F9` - Paper Tint):** Deployed for subtle background stratification, recessed data modules, and table alternating rows.
- **Base Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`):** Provide expansive canvas clarity, keeping deep-nested forms uncluttered.
- **Border / Stroke (`#CBD5E1` - Muted Slate):** Creates crisp delineations across cards, tables, and modal divisions without adding visual noise.

## Typography

The typographic system joins **Manrope** for authoritative, geometric display surfaces with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for exceptionally legible operational text and UI microcopy.

- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric structure and steady proportions lend high institutional credibility. Dense letter tracking in larger sizes (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`) prevents optical float on dashboard title bars and public portal landing views.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with balanced internal counters and humanist terminals, ensuring effortless comprehension across financial balance sheets, ledger entries, and audit timestamps.
- **Numerical Rigor:** For tables, balances, and operational counters, pair `label-md` and `body-md` with tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during data streaming or live ledger reconciliation.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a structural **fluid-responsive 12-column grid** anchored to an 8px base rhythm (with 4px micro-steps for tight form controls):

- **Desktop (1280px and wider):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, 2rem (`32px`) lateral margins. Max canvas width constrained to 1440px for single-pane dashboards, or 100% fluid within authenticated dual-sidebar console shells.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1.5rem (`24px`) margins. Sidebar transforms into a collapsible rail or overlay drawer. Secondary data splits fold from side-by-side into stacked tabs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1rem (`16px`) margins. Multi-column metric sets collapse to horizontal swipe shelves or full-width stacked KPI cards.
- **Component Padding Standards:** Dense tables use `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally; cards and executive metric modules standardize on `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **tonal layering** and **low-contrast micro-outlines**, deliberately eschewing heavy drop shadows to project modern digital governance clarity:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Plain `#F8FAFC` background. Never elevated; establishes ground state.
- **Level 1 (Structural Containers & Cards):** Flat `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a crisp `1px solid #CBD5E1` border. Under gentle interaction or hover, apply a diffused tint shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers, Segment Filters):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #CBD5E1` and an elevated shadow: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Drawers):** Bounded by a soft semi-transparent overlay `#0F172A` at 40% opacity, utilizing `0 20px 32px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)`.
- **Submerged States (Recessed inputs, code snippets, audit log shells):** `#F1F5F9` background container with an inset shadow `inset 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)**, projecting contemporary approachability while honoring governmental order:

- **Base Components (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem - 8px):** Standard buttons, text input fields, dropdown trigger buttons, and badge chips.
- **Structural Modules (`rounded-lg` / 1rem - 16px):** Standard data cards, dashboard analytic panels, table container wrappers, and form section blocks.
- **Focal Windows (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem - 24px):** Executive summary banners, dialog modals, system drawer trays, and hero milestone spotlights.
- **Full Radius (Pill / 9999px):** Status badges, count tags, and floating action markers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#1E3A8A` with `#FFFFFF` text. Solid state, subtle transition on hover to `#172554`. Focus ring: 2px offset with 2px stroke in `#3B82F6`. Border radius: `rounded-md` (`8px`).
- **Secondary (Action Utility):** Outlined `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, `#0F172A` text, `#FFFFFF` background. Hover brings a shift to `#F1F5F9` and `#1E3A8A` text.
- **Accent Milestone (Highlight / CTA):** Filled `#D97706` with `#FFFFFF` text. Deployed strictly for pivotal actions like "Approve Ledger", "Disburse Grant", or "Finalize Audit". Hover transitions to `#B45309`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No background, `#3B82F6` text, underline on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Verified / Active:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E3A8A`, subtle border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
- **Milestone / Attention Alert:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Neutral / Draft:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Shape is consistently pill-formed with `label-sm` uppercase tracking.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Background `#FFFFFF`, resting border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, typography `body-md` in `#0F172A`.
- Focused state transforms border to `#3B82F6` accompanied by an ambient focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.18)`.
- Helper labels sit above in `label-md` `#0F172A`. Disabled fields drop to `#F1F5F9` surface with `#94A3B8` text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px dimension, `rounded` (`4px` for checkbox, pill for radio), resting border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Checked state fills with `#1E3A8A` showing a crisp white icon checkmark. Keyboard focus manifests the standard `#3B82F6` glow ring.

### Cards & Data Panels
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Corner radius: `rounded-lg` (`16px`).
- Card headers feature an optional internal border-bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9` with `title-md` typography.
- Metric cards display numerical values in `headline-lg` (`Manrope`) with comparative percentage change chips anchored top-right.

### Tables & Data Grids
- Wrapped in `rounded-lg` with outer border `#CBD5E1`. Header row uses `#F8FAFC` surface with uppercase `label-xs` tracking.
- Row division is governed by `1px solid #F1F5F9`, transitioning to `#F8FAFC` on hover. Active selection applies a left accent border in `#1E3A8A` (3px thickness).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Audit Verification Seal:** A dual-ring badge combining `#1E3A8A` with `#3B82F6` vector iconography and cryptographic hash truncation in monospaced numbers.
- **Milestone Progress Stepper:** Horizontal tracking rail in `#E2E8F0`, filled sequentially with `#D97706` indicators and completed nodes in `#1E3A8A`.
