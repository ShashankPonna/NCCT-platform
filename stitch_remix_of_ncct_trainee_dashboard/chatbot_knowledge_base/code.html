Programme Detail

The Certification in Basic Computer Literacy is a 6-week program designed for absolute beginners. It covers fundamental operating system navigation, basic word processing, and safe internet browsing practices. The course requires no prior experience and provides a foundational certificate upon passing the final practical assessment.

General FAQ

Password resets can be initiated by clicking the "Forgot Password" link on the main login screen. A reset link will be sent to the registered email address. If the email is not received within 15 minutes, users should check their spam folder before contacting support. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM.

Policy Document

Attendance Policy: Learners must maintain a minimum of 80% attendance for all scheduled synchronous sessions to be eligible for final certification. Extenuating circumstances must be communicated to the program coordinator within 48 hours of an absence, accompanied by relevant documentation (e.g., medical certificate).
